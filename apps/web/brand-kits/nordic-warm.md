## Mood
- warm scandinavian
- hygge, lived-in comfort
- honest and unpretentious
- soft and airy
- everyday calm

## Materials
- light oak and ash with visible grain
- white-painted timber panelling
- chunky wool and mohair throws
- washed linen in oatmeal and sand
- matte off-white ceramics
- brushed pale brass, lightly aged

## Palette
- base: chalk white, oatmeal, pale oak, soft sand
- accent: muted terracotta, sage green, pale clay
- avoid: pure black, high-contrast monochrome, glossy surfaces, saturated red

## Lighting
- abundant soft northern daylight, diffused and even
- no direct sun patches, no hard shadow edges
- bright airy exposure with gentle contrast
- cool grey daylight outdoors visible through windows

## Composition
- wide interior photograph, 35mm equivalent
- straight-on or gently oblique eye-level view
- furniture arranged low and loose, lots of breathing room
- visible floor and ceiling to establish the room
- honest, unstyled framing rather than a catalogue setup

## Styling
- a few dried stems in a simple glass vase
- a folded wool throw over the sofa arm
- one open book or a pair of reading glasses
- a single stoneware mug
- natural fibre rug, undyed

## Never
- visible logos, brand marks or text overlays
- plastic surfaces or high-gloss lacquer
- clutter, crowded shelves or busy patterns
- cold blue-grey tint over the whole image
- artificial CGI perfection or plastic-looking plants

## Notes
面向北欧风格灯具的欧美独立站。关键是"松"：
家具低、留白多、光线均匀、材质哑光。
注意别做成宜家目录——要有真实生活的松弛感，不要过度陈列。
