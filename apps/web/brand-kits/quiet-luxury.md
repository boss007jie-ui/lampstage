## Mood
- quiet luxury
- understated and expensive
- calm, unhurried, editorial
- hotel-suite composure
- low visual noise

## Materials
- honed travertine and limestone
- warm greige plaster walls
- unlacquered antique brass details
- ivory bouclé and heavy linen
- dark walnut with visible grain
- matte ceramic and stoneware

## Palette
- base: ivory, warm greige, limestone, soft taupe
- accent: antique brass, deep walnut, muted olive
- avoid: neon, saturated primaries, pure black, pure white, cool blue cast

## Lighting
- soft directional morning daylight from one tall window
- gentle falloff into the room's deeper corners
- warm low-contrast shadows, no harsh specular highlights
- overall exposure slightly bright, never blown out

## Composition
- wide editorial interior photograph, 35mm equivalent
- eye-level or slightly elevated oblique angle
- room and furniture fill most of the frame
- generous negative space on the walls
- symmetrical furniture groupings where the architecture allows

## Styling
- one sculptural ceramic vessel, one hardback book
- single olive or eucalyptus branch, never a bouquet
- sheer linen curtain partially drawn
- visible craft: hand-thrown edges, woven texture, plaster imperfections

## Never
- visible logos, brand marks or text overlays
- plastic or glossy chrome surfaces
- cluttered surfaces or crowded furniture
- artificial-looking CGI gloss or over-sharpened HDR
- colourful patterned rugs or busy wallpapers

## Notes
面向欧美独立站/品牌官网的高客单价灯具。核心是"贵而不吵"：
用材质和留白表达价格，而不是用对比度和饱和度。
和 modern_luxury 客群关键词（黑金、大理石、皮革）刻意区分——那套偏"显性的奢华"，
这套偏"隐性的高级"，两者不应混在同一批出图里。
