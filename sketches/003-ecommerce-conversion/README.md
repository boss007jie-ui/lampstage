## Variant: Ecommerce Conversion

### Design stance
A sales-driven page for domestic ecommerce merchants: direct value claims, high-contrast CTA, and obvious cost-saving language.

### Key choices
- Layout: Hero copy + interactive demo + stats + three-step process.
- Typography: Bold Inter with ecommerce urgency.
- Color: Warm orange/yellow, dark brown, strong CTA contrast.
- Interaction: Free-generation CTA toast.

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: Explaining business value quickly to Taobao/1688 sellers.
- Weak at: Less premium and less calm than the brand/tool variants.

### Best for
- Paid ads, domestic seller acquisition, landing pages focused on conversion.
