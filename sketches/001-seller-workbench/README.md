## Variant: Seller Workbench

### Design stance
A practical SaaS workspace for lamp sellers: upload and template selection are always visible, while the generated ecommerce scene preview dominates the right side.

### Key choices
- Layout: Sidebar + settings panel + large preview canvas.
- Typography: Vercel-inspired Geist, clean and tool-like.
- Color: Warm neutrals and amber light accents to fit lighting products.
- Interaction: Template selection, platform style toggle, and generation toast.

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: Making the core workflow obvious and controllable.
- Weak at: Less emotional marketing impact than a landing-page-first direction.

### Best for
- Sellers who want to log in and quickly produce product scene images every day.
