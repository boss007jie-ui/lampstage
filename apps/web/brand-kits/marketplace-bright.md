## Mood
- bright and clean
- immediately legible at thumbnail size
- tidy, aspirational, commercial
- modern Chinese home
- high clarity, low ambiguity

## Materials
- pale oak veneer and warm white lacquer
- matte porcelain tile flooring
- brushed champagne metal accents
- smooth microfibre and cotton blends
- tempered glass and marble-effect surfaces

## Palette
- base: warm white, light beige, pale oak, soft grey
- accent: champagne gold, muted teal, dusty rose
- avoid: heavy dark woods, neon, muddy browns, cold fluorescent cast

## Lighting
- even bright daylight, evenly distributed across the whole frame
- no dark corners, no heavy shadow anywhere
- overall exposure bright and clean, whites reading as clean white
- gentle warm undertone throughout

## Composition
- wide to medium-wide interior view, 35mm equivalent
- slightly elevated oblique angle showing floor and ceiling
- furniture complete and tidy, everything squared to the room
- clean uncluttered background surfaces
- subject placement clearly off the frame edges

## Styling
- one neat stack of two or three books
- a simple vase with fresh green leaves
- a folded throw in a coordinating tone
- a small tray with a cup
- minimal, symmetrical, nothing out of place

## Never
- visible logos, brand marks, text overlays or watermarks
- clutter, laundry, cables or everyday mess
- dark moody corners or heavy vignetting
- yellow or green colour cast on white surfaces
- reflections of camera equipment or studio rigs

## Notes
面向国内电商主图/详情页场景。核心是"清楚"：
缩略图里一眼能看懂房间是什么、产品在什么位置。
亮度更高、家具更整齐、颜色更干净，和品牌氛围版刻意拉开差异——
同一批素材不要混用这两种风格，否则商家会觉得"风格不统一"。
