# Ecommerce keyword optimizer

Input: product brief, target marketplace or storefront, locale, keyword constraints and desired tone.

Return:

1. title candidates with a short rationale
2. five to seven selling points grounded in confirmed facts
3. primary and secondary keyword groups
4. localized description with prohibited or uncertain claims called out

Respect channel length limits supplied by the user. Keep keywords natural and never add search terms that misrepresent the product.
