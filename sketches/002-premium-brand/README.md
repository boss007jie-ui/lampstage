## Variant: Premium Brand

### Design stance
A polished landing-first experience that makes the product feel premium and trustworthy before users enter the generator.

### Key choices
- Layout: Marketing hero + visual scene + simple workflow sections.
- Typography: Stripe-inspired light headings and navy text.
- Color: White, deep navy, purple CTA, warm lamp glow.
- Interaction: CTA toast that points users into the generator.

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: Selling the product to higher-value clients and independent-store sellers.
- Weak at: The actual tool workflow is less visible on the first screen.

### Best for
- Acquisition pages, ads, and sellers who need trust before trying an AI tool.
